WhatsApp groups

The agent sits in a WhatsApp group like any member. It tracks every message, notices requirements on its own (text or voice, any language), takes them forward, asks the sender what is missing, and brings the group admins the decision. Nobody has to type a command.

Unofficial connection. WhatsApp does not allow bots in ordinary groups, so this logs in as a normal WhatsApp account (a spare number, scanned once by QR). That is against WhatsApp's terms and the number can be banned: demo use only.
